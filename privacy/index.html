INFNTY Publisher — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

INFNTY Publisher and INFNTY Shorts ("the apps") are internal tools operated by INFNTY to publish videos created by INFNTY to INFNTY's own TikTok account (@infnty.cltr) and INFNTY's own YouTube channel. The apps are not available to the public.

Information we access. When the account owner signs in with TikTok or Google, the apps receive only what is needed for the permissions granted: basic profile or channel information (ID, display name) and an access token that allows uploading videos to that account (TikTok user.info.basic and video.upload; YouTube youtube.upload and youtube.readonly).

How we use it. This information is used only to upload INFNTY's own videos and captions to INFNTY's own TikTok account and YouTube channel and to check the status of those uploads.

Google user data. INFNTY Shorts' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are used under the YouTube Terms of Service (https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and the Google Privacy Policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Storage. Access tokens are stored only on the account owner's own computer and are never published or sold. We do not collect information about other users or viewers.

Sharing. We do not share, sell or rent any information to third parties. Videos are sent only to TikTok and YouTube through their official APIs.

Retention and deletion. Tokens are kept only as long as publishing is active. Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok's settings (Settings and privacy → Security → Manage app permissions) or from https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. We delete stored tokens on request.
