INFNTY — infinity culture

INFNTY is a community for people who love, live and grow through every form of art: music, fashion, painting and writing. Not a brand, a movement.

On Instagram, TikTok and YouTube (@infnty.cltr) we publish short educational videos about electronic music, DJ culture and music history, and a weekly "Under the Radar" recommendation of tracks the algorithm has not caught yet.

INFNTY Publisher is the small in-house desktop tool we use to publish our own videos to our own @infnty.cltr TikTok account. It is not offered to other users.
